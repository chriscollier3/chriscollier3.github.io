Senior capstone design begins Fall 2026. The project, team, and clinical sponsor are to be determined. Check back as the year progresses — this page will be updated with full design documentation, prototyping progress, and results as the project develops.

Senior capstone design is the culminating engineering experience of the Biomedical Engineering program at the University of Delaware. Teams work with clinical sponsors to identify a real unmet need, develop and prototype a solution through the full medical device design process, and present their work at the end of the year. The project integrates skills from across the curriculum — engineering design, biomechanics, materials, regulatory, and clinical context — into a single year-long deliverable.

I'm entering this experience with a background in medical device fabrication from my work at the BRITE Lab, familiarity with the design process from my Teaching Assistant role in Medical Device Design, and a strong interest in applying those skills to a problem with direct clinical impact. More details will be posted here once the project is underway.
