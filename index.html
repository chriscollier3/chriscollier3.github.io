About Me

I'm a Biomedical Engineering student at the University of Delaware with a minor in Biomechanical Engineering, and a strong foundation in medical device design, CAD/prototyping, and biomechanical research. I approach engineering problems through the full design cycle — from concept generation and 3D modeling to hands-on fabrication and validation testing — focused on fully meeting clinical needs.

Research & Design

Projects

Engineering designs stretching from medical device design and development, computational modeling, and biomechanical analysis.

01 — Engineering Research & Development

Microfluidic Membrane Oxygenator

Developing a PDMS-on-glass composite flat plate membrane oxygenator for blood gas exchange in the BRITE Lab. Device work includes 3D-printed polycarbonate flow cell fabrication modeled in OnShape, along with a rigorous verification and validation testing framework covering external leak detection via pressure hold and submersion, trans-membrane integrity via dye perfusion, and bubble point testing. Fluid dynamic analysis addresses internal shear stress distributions and flow uniformity across the membrane surface to ensure physiologically safe operating conditions.

OnShape PDMS Fabrication 3D Prototyping Fluid Dynamics V&V Testing Biomaterials
02 — Engineering Research & Development

Hollow Fiber Membrane Oxygenator

Designing a scalable oxygenator using stacked Oxyplus PMP hollow fiber membrane sheets, with iterative 3D CAD modeling and physical prototyping in OnShape and FDM 3D printing to develop and refine device geometry. Work involved systematic evaluation of bonding and sealing strategies, ultimately converging on a centrifugal potting method with bent fiber geometry. This solution eliminates corner flow stasis in the blood path, preserves fiber lumen integrity during potting, and produces a curved open flow channel — independently developed and subsequently validated against established industrial techniques.

OnShape 3D Printing Centrifugal Potting Hollow Fiber Membranes Design Iteration
03 — Junior Design

Radial Arterial Catheterization Wrist Support

Junior design project addressing patient comfort and procedural consistency during radial arterial line placement. Developed a wrist support device that maintains optimal wrist extension angle and stabilizes the insertion site. The design emphasizes structural adaptability — accommodating variation in patient anatomy while maintaining reliable catheter access geometry for clinical staff across a range of use conditions.

SolidWorks 3D Printing Laser Cutting Soldering Medical Device Design
04 — Pharmaceutical Modeling

Pharmacokinetic Modeling of Propofol: Three-Compartment Simulation

Built a three-compartment pharmacokinetic simulation of Propofol in MATLAB, modeling drug distribution across plasma, peripheral tissues, and a deep tissue compartment following IV administration. The simulation captures redistribution kinetics and elimination dynamics, producing plasma concentration-time profiles relevant to computational and compartmental modeling of drug behavior in clinical pharmacology.

MATLAB ODE Modeling Compartmental Modeling Computational Modeling
05 — Clinical Biomechanics

Squatting Biomechanics Analysis

Analyzed lower extremity joint mechanics during squat patterns for a clinical biomechanics course, examining joint loading, muscle activation strategies, and kinematic variation across individuals using OpenCap motion analysis. Statistical analysis was performed in JMP. Findings were evaluated in the context of rehabilitation protocol design and injury prevention, with implications for load management in clinical and athletic populations.

OpenCap Motion Analysis JMP Biostatistics Biomechanics Joint Kinetics
