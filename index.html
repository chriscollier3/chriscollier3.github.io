Microfluidic Membrane Oxygenator

Developing a PDMS-on-glass composite flat plate membrane oxygenator for blood gas exchange applications. Work includes device fabrication, leak detection protocols (pressure hold, dye perfusion, bubble point testing), and channel geometry characterization using modified Hagen-Poiseuille analysis for rectangular microchannels.

Hollow Fiber Membrane Oxygenator

Designing a scalable oxygenator using stacked Oxyplus PMP hollow fiber membrane sheets. Key contributions include systematic evaluation of bonding and sealing strategies, and development of a centrifugal potting approach with bent fiber geometry to eliminate corner flow stasis and preserve fiber lumen integrity — an approach independently converged upon and validated against established industrial methods.

Radial Arterial Catheterization Wrist Support

Junior design project focused on improving patient comfort and catheter access during radial arterial line placement. Developed a wrist support device that maintains optimal wrist extension angle, stabilizes the insertion site, and reduces procedural variability for clinical staff.
