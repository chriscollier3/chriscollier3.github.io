Projects

Engineering work spanning medical device development, physiological modeling, and biomechanics.

01 — Lab Research

Microfluidic Membrane Oxygenator

Developing a PDMS-on-glass composite flat plate membrane oxygenator for blood gas exchange. Device work includes 3D-printed polycarbonate flow cell fabrication modeled in OnShape, along with a rigorous verification and validation testing framework covering external leak detection via pressure hold and submersion, trans-membrane integrity via dye perfusion, and bubble point testing. Fluid dynamic analysis addresses internal shear stress distributions and flow uniformity across the membrane surface to ensure physiologically safe operating conditions.

OnShape CAD PDMS Fabrication 3D Printing Fluid Dynamics V&V Testing
02 — Lab Research

Hollow Fiber Membrane Oxygenator

Designing a scalable oxygenator using stacked Oxyplus PMP hollow fiber membrane sheets, with iterative 3D CAD modeling in OnShape to prototype and refine device geometry. Work involved systematic evaluation of bonding and sealing strategies across multiple fabrication approaches, ultimately converging on a centrifugal potting method with bent fiber geometry. This solution eliminates corner flow stasis in the blood path, preserves fiber lumen integrity during potting, and produces a curved open flow channel — independently developed and subsequently validated against established industrial techniques.

OnShape CAD Centrifugal Potting Hollow Fiber Membranes 3D Prototyping Design Iteration
03 — Junior Design

Radial Arterial Catheterization Wrist Support

Junior design project addressing patient comfort and procedural consistency during radial arterial line placement. Developed a wrist support device that maintains optimal wrist extension angle and stabilizes the insertion site. The design emphasizes structural adaptability — accommodating variation in patient anatomy while maintaining reliable catheter access geometry for clinical staff across a range of use conditions.

Medical Device Design CAD Prototyping Clinical Application
04 — Computational Modeling

Pharmacokinetic Modeling of Propofol: Three-Compartment Simulation

Built a three-compartment pharmacokinetic simulation of Propofol in MATLAB, modeling drug distribution across plasma, peripheral tissues, and a deep tissue compartment following IV administration. The simulation captures redistribution kinetics and elimination dynamics, producing plasma concentration-time profiles relevant to anesthesia dosing analysis and illustrating the narrow window between therapeutic and toxic exposure levels.

MATLAB ODE Modeling Pharmacokinetics Anesthesia
05 — Clinical Biomechanics

Squatting Biomechanics Analysis

Analyzed lower extremity joint mechanics during squat patterns for a clinical biomechanics course, examining joint loading, muscle activation strategies, and kinematic variation across individuals. Findings were evaluated in the context of rehabilitation protocol design and injury prevention, with implications for load management in clinical and athletic populations.

Biomechanics Motion Analysis Rehabilitation Joint Kinetics
