Microfluidic Membrane Oxygenator

Developing a PDMS-on-glass composite flat plate membrane oxygenator for blood gas exchange. Device work includes 3D-printed polycarbonate flow cell fabrication modeled in OnShape, along with a rigorous verification and validation testing framework covering external leak detection via pressure hold and submersion, trans-membrane integrity via dye perfusion, bubble point testing, and channel geometry characterization. Fluid dynamic analysis addresses internal shear stress distributions and flow uniformity across the membrane surface to ensure physiologically safe operating conditions.

Hollow Fiber Membrane Oxygenator

Designing a scalable oxygenator using stacked Oxyplus PMP hollow fiber membrane sheets, with 3D CAD modeling in OnShape to prototype and iterate on device geometry. Work involved systematic evaluation of bonding and sealing strategies across multiple fabrication approaches, ultimately converging on a centrifugal potting method with bent fiber geometry. This solution eliminates corner flow stasis in the blood path, preserves fiber lumen integrity during potting, and produces a curved open flow channel — independently developed and subsequently validated against established industrial techniques.

Radial Arterial Catheterization Wrist Support

Junior design project addressing patient comfort and procedural consistency during radial arterial line placement. Developed a wrist support device that maintains optimal wrist extension angle and stabilizes the insertion site. The design emphasizes structural adaptability — accommodating variation in patient anatomy while maintaining reliable catheter access geometry for clinical staff across a range of use conditions.
